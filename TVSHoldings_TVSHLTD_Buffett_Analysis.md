> **Sector Detection: STANDARD MODE (Investment / Holding Company)**
>
> TVS Holdings Ltd is a **Core Investment Company (CIC)** registered with the RBI (March 2024). While classified as an NBFC-CIC, its economic substance is that of a **holding company** — it does not originate loans, take deposits, or conduct banking operations itself. Its consolidated financials are driven overwhelmingly by its **50.26% stake in TVS Motor Company** and the latter's subsidiary **TVS Credit Services** (an NBFC). STANDARD MODE is applied to the consolidated entity, with supplementary NBFC commentary where TVS Credit's financials are material.

> **Valuation Philosophy:** This analysis follows a pure Buffett/Munger philosophy. EBITDA-based metrics (EV/EBITDA, Debt/EBITDA) are excluded. EBIT is tracked throughout. Owner Earnings is the primary cash flow metric. P/Owner Earnings is the preferred valuation multiple. Discount rate is 10% flat (Buffett's hurdle rate). Screener.in's 'Operating Profit' is EBITDA-equivalent (pre-D&A); EBIT = Operating Profit − D&A is computed and tracked separately.

> **Important — Consolidated vs Attributable:** TVS Holdings owns ~50.26% of TVS Motor. Consolidated NP includes minority shareholders' ~50% share. EPS figures from Screener use **attributable** profit only. This ~2x gap between consolidated NP and EPS×Shares is a structural feature of holding company accounting, noted where it matters.

## 1. THE BUSINESS — Understanding the Economic Engine

> *"Never invest in a business you cannot understand."* — Warren Buffett

**TVS Holdings Ltd** (formerly Sundaram-Clayton Ltd) is the Venu Srinivasan family's apex holding entity for the TVS Group. The company underwent a major restructuring in 2022-23 — the die-casting manufacturing business was demerged to a separate entity (Sundaram-Clayton Ltd), and the residual entity was renamed TVS Holdings Ltd. It received CIC registration from the RBI in March 2024 and wound down its spare parts trading business by October 2024.

**What does TVS Holdings actually own?**

| Holding | Entity | Nature | Approx. Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **50.26%** | **TVS Motor Company Ltd** | India's 3rd-largest two-wheeler manufacturer | ~₹82,000 Cr (at market) |
| Via TVSM | TVS Credit Services Ltd | NBFC — 2W, used cars, tractors financing; AUM ~₹29,000 Cr | Unlisted; est. ₹8,000-12,000 Cr |
| Via TVSM | Norton Motorcycles (UK) | Premium motorcycle brand | Early stage |
| Via TVSM | Swiss E-Mobility Group | E-bikes in Europe | Growing |
| Direct | Home Credit India Finance | Acquired NBFC | ~₹1,500-2,000 Cr |
| Direct | Other investments | Various group entities | Modest |

**The Buffett Lens:** At its core, TVS Holdings is a **toll bridge on Indian two-wheeler mobility**. It owns the single largest stake in TVS Motor Company, which manufactures motorcycles, scooters, mopeds, three-wheelers, and EVs — products every Indian understands. The captive financing arm (TVS Credit) finances purchases of these vehicles. The business model is elegantly simple: **India's 1.4 billion people need affordable personal transportation, and TVS Motor sells it to them while TVS Credit finances it.**

> *"I want to be in businesses so good that even a fool can run them. Because sooner or later, one will."* — Buffett. TVS Motor itself passes this test — two-wheelers are a consumer franchise with strong brand loyalty, distribution moats, and high repeat purchase rates. The holding company is simply the wrapper.
